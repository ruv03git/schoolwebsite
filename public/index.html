Our Learning Programs

Tailored developmental stages for every child

Playgroup (Age 2 - 3 yrs)

Sensory exploration, motor skills development, social interaction, and fun group play to build comfort away from home.

Nursery (Age 3 - 4 yrs)

Language building, rhythm, numbers awareness, self-expression, and habit formation through joyful storytelling and crafts.

LKG (Age 3.5 / 4 - 5 yrs)

Phonics, pre-writing, numeracy concepts, cognitive thinking, and social collaboration in cheerful classrooms.

UKG (Age 4.5 / 5 - 6 yrs)

Primary school readiness, reading fluency, basic arithmetic, scientific curiosity, and confidence building.
